The Brand

KING — Authenticity in Every Piece

KING is a brand that holds quality as its defining standard. In the 500-piece puzzle collection, timeless works of art and much-loved designs are turned into puzzles with careful craftsmanship and close attention to detail, creating a reliable, valuable product for retailers, distributors and puzzle enthusiasts alike.

THE COLLECTION

KING 500 Pieces Puzzle

KING 500-piece puzzles combine quality construction, precise cutting and high-resolution printing to deliver a genuinely enjoyable puzzling experience. A wide variety of designs makes the collection an attractive choice for every taste and interest.
